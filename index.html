Fixtures.

Everyone plays everyone in their group. Fifteen matches per court, nobody plays back to back.

    Standings.

    Top four in each group go through to the quarter-finals.

    The road to the trophy.

    Knockouts are best of three games to 21. Group winners are kept apart until the final.

    How it works.

    Simple format. Zero mercy.

    Players

    Twelve pairs, twenty-four players.

    Groups

    Two groups of six.

    Each group owns its court. No mixing.

    Group stage

    Fifteen matches per group.

    Every pair plays all five rivals. One game to 21, no deuce.

    Top four go through.

    Ranked by wins, then point difference, then points scored, then head-to-head. The bottom two in each group are out.
